Need More Help?

Welcome to the West End Café Help Center! Here you’ll find answers to common questions about your account, orders, payments, and more.

☕ 1. Account & Logins
Having trouble signing in or creating an account?

  • Make sure your email and password are correct.
  • If you’ve forgotten your password, click “Forgot Password” on the login page to reset it.
  • Didn’t receive your confirmation email? Check your spam/junk folder.
Still having issues? Contact us at westandchillers@gmail.com .

💳 2. Payments & Receipts
  • We accept cash, card, and mobile payments.
  • Always request and keep your receipt or proof of payment.
  • If you notice any billing issues, please contact us within 24 hours of purchase.

🔄 3. Refund Policy
    Our refund policy ensures fairness for all customers:
  • A valid receipt or proof of payment is required for all refunds.
  • Failure to collect an order means no refund will be issued.
  • Refunds are processed only with management approval.
Read our full Refund Policy for more details.

🎟️ 4. Events & Tickets
Tickets for special events can be purchased in-store or via Computicket. Please double-check event details before purchase — tickets are non-refundable unless an event is cancelled by West End Café.
