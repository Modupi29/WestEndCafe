Refund Policy

At West End Café, we value our customers and strive to provide quality service at all times. Please take note of our refund policy:

Proof of Payment:
Refunds will only be processed if valid proof of payment (such as a receipt or payment confirmation) is provided.

Receipts:
Customers are required to present their original receipt when requesting a refund or exchange.
Failure to Collect Orders:
Orders that have already been processed will not be eligible for a refund. Should you wish to cancel your order, call an hour prior to the collection time.
Management Discretion:
All refunds are subject to approval by management and processed according to café policy.
